# Install Oarwise

**English** · [简体中文](installation.zh-CN.md)

## 1. Download

On your Windows PC, open the [Oarwise account page](https://oarwise.com/account) and sign in. If this is your first time, activate your account using the code you purchased.

Choose the Codex, DSH, or Skill installer for your AI client. The Skill package requires a client that supports installing Skills on your PC. A regular browser chat cannot install it directly.

## 2. Extract with the password from your email

Open the download with 7-Zip and enter the password sent to your account email address.

Extraction gives you another archive. Keep it as is for the next step. If you did not receive a password, select **Resend password** on your account page.

## 3. Ask your AI client to install it

Drag the resulting archive into a chat in the matching AI client and say the following.

> Please install Oarwise for me.

Follow the prompts and confirm the required permissions. Keep the extraction password out of the chat.

If your AI client cannot accept archive attachments, save the file to your PC and give the AI its full path. DSH users can also select the archive in Plugin Manager.

## 4. Connect your PC

On the PC you want to connect, start a new local chat with the plugin enabled and say the following.

> Call Oarwise's account_login and give me the verification link for this PC.

Open the link the AI gives you, sign in with your activated Oarwise account, and confirm the connection. The link fills in the connection code for you.

Return to the chat and ask the AI to check `account_status`. Once authorization succeeds, tell the AI which window to use and what you want it to do.

For installation or account help, contact the shop with your order number. Keep activation codes, verification codes, and extraction passwords private.
